This page supplies the sample context for [Quick Start](/en/docs/quick-start/). It helps you recognise
the relationship between evidence, a process, a product system, and LCIA. **It does not claim that
the platform reproduces the paper's model or conclusions, and it is not an import-ready package.**

## Sample source

[Comparative environmental assessment of methanol production technologies: A cradle-to-gate life cycle analysis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enconman.2024.118128)

## Written observation route

1. Record the paper title and DOI as external evidence, not an automatic source of platform data.
2. Search Open Data for `methanol`; if there is no exact match, choose any visible process.
3. In process detail, locate the quantitative reference, reference flow, and exchanges. A process
   reference quantity is not automatically the study functional unit.
4. Open an available LCIA result and record the method, version or snapshot, factor evidence, and
   coverage state.
5. Use Process Analysis to inspect contribution evidence. The practitioner remains responsible for
   study conclusions, sensitivity judgments, and the final report.

You can complete the written route without video playback.
